SmartStock Linux

Extract the entire archive, open a terminal in the extracted directory, and run:

  ./install.sh

Run as your normal user, without sudo. Then open SmartStock from your application
menu. The installer copies the entire bundle under ~/.local/share/smartstock and
installs the launcher and icons. It respects XDG_DATA_HOME when set. Close the
running app before installing an updated bundle. Application data is not removed.

You can also run ./smartstock_flutter directly from the extracted bundle.
Keep lib/ and data/ beside the executable. This build was made on CachyOS x64;
older distributions may not provide sufficiently recent system libraries.
The app requires GTK 3, libsecret, and a running, unlocked Secret Service keyring.
